The order, in plain terms

Four things every listing must show

Office Order FS/SD/A/O/2026-27/838 directs all retail pharmacies to maintain a public website with these details, kept current week to week. Hospital pharmacies must also display a price list based on actual purchase and sale rates.

Enforcement begins 1 November 2026. Drug inspectors are directed to verify compliance within their jurisdiction.
  • Licence numberYour drug sale licence, displayed verbatim.
  • Appointed pharmacistName and registration of the pharmacist on record.
  • Licensed premisesThe address the licence was issued against.
  • Stock detailsItem, batch, expiry, quantity and MRP — weekly.

How it works

No backend. No subscription. No lock-in.

Rojaco is a static site. Your data is a single JSON file you own, in a repository you control.

  1. Add your store

    Describe your pharmacy once in data/stores.json — licence, pharmacist, premises and contact.

  2. Update stock weekly

    Edit the items list with item, batch, expiry, quantity and MRP. Commit and push.

  3. Share the link

    Your page goes live at /stores/<slug>/ on GitHub Pages, or on your own domain.

  4. Print for inspection

    The page is styled for A4. Hit print and hand over a clean, signed stock statement.
